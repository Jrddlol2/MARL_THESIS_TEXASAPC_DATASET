# Weather-dataset figures — captions

- **fig_weather_precip.png** — Daily precipitation at Camp Mabry (NOAA LCDv2) across the July–December 2021 study window; rain is episodic, concentrated in a few storm days.
- **fig_weather_exposure.png** — Weather exposure of the cleaned events: 11,804 of 229,421 stop-events (5.1%) occurred under observed rain.
- **fig_weather_traveltime.png** — Descriptive median segment time, dry (204 s) vs rain (212 s). UNADJUSTED and NOT a causal weather effect — a multiplier requires segment, time-of-day, and day-type controls (MSA2). The simulator's weather stressor remains synthetic.
- **fig_weather_join.png** — Join validity: 100% of 229,421 events matched a NOAA reading within 90 minutes (median gap 12.7 min); the two stations agree on the rain flag for 94% of events.
