What this is
  The full LaTeX compile set for the revised manuscript that goes with
  Conformity of Revisions No. 2 and the letter of request to the RTC.
  Upload this folder to Overleaf as-is (main.tex is the entry point).

Where it comes from

  of 2026-10-21), plus two edits made on 2026-11-06:

  1. problem.tex, Section 2.3.1 Main Objective, now reads:
       "To evaluate a Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning controller for
        dynamic bus scheduling under baseline and non-ideal operating
        conditions."
     (the wording proposed to the Exam Chair; conformity entry P2)

  2. methods.tex, Section 3.2.6 Weather-Induced Anomalies: one paragraph
     added after "...correspond to Austin rainfall categories." It states
     that the Espino et al. (2018) rainfall coefficients [9] are not
     adopted as model parameters (answers the Reference 10 question,
     conformity item 13).

  ("...schedule timepoints, while the direction-code-6 subset modelled here
  comprises the 29 distinct stop IDs served between and including them.").
  The October PDF printed it cut off after "are schedule".
